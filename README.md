An entry for the make.fun contest "The Painfully Honest Out-of-Office" — one brutally honest but still polite out-of-office auto-reply, in a single sentence of at most 200 characters. The program `main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only, no network, no randomness) deterministically writes the reply to `/output/entry.txt`: "I'm away until Monday, and your email has joined the 847 unread messages I will open with genuine enthusiasm and absolutely no plan — thank you for your patience." It admits the inbox is a mountain the sender will only ever tackle with enthusiasm and no plan, then stays polite enough that a manager would let it stand.
